Every site, one number — and the ticket knows where it came from.

A WhatsApp-first support desk for the MEC estate. A site lead messages one number; Zoho Desk identifies the location, triages the fault, and hands an agent a ready-to-work ticket — with global oversight built in for Keme.

How it works

01

Lead reports a fault

A site lead messages the support WhatsApp number — the channel they already use — or emails the single support address.

02

Desk knows the site

The sender's number matches their contact record, so the ticket is tagged to that location automatically. No "which site, which room?"

03

Bot triages first

Guided questions capture the system, symptom and severity before an agent is ever involved.

04

Routed + Keme alerted

A fully-tagged ticket lands in the shared queue, and Keme is notified in parallel — every time.

Why it works

  • Single front door. Leads use the channel they're already on, with one email address as a backup route — no app to learn, no portal to remember.
  • Location without asking. The number identifies the site, so every ticket is tagged before a word is typed.
  • Triage before a human. Agents open tickets that already say where, what and how bad — faster first response, fewer back-and-forths.
  • One queue, scales cleanly. Two or three agents cover the whole estate; the plan allows unlimited agents, so growth adds no per-seat surprises.
  • Global oversight, your way. Keme is alerted on every ticket with the site in the subject line — or switch to a single daily summary so oversight stays signal, not noise.Daily digest option
  • Near-zero messaging cost. Inbound support sits inside WhatsApp's free 24-hour service window; realistic Meta spend is about $0.
  • Nothing to host. Runs entirely inside Zoho Desk, and Keme needs no licence to receive alerts.
